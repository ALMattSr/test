Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up an appointment?

Call 479-876-8626 and leave your name and number, email valerie@askwellnesscenter.com, or use the request form below. We’ll get back to you within 24–48 hours.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept many insurance plans, including EAP and Medicare, as well as private pay and credit or debit cards. Call ahead and we’ll check your specific plan.

How long are sessions, and how often will I come?

Sessions are usually 50–60 minutes. How often you come depends on what you’re working on; most people return within about two weeks. Existing clients can request appointments through our online portal, and we’re happy to help if you need it.

Can I be seen after work or school?

Yes. We offer appointments from 9 am to 9 pm, Monday through Friday, with some Saturday times available. Sessions can be in our office or by secure telehealth.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. All records and communications are safeguarded in line with HIPAA and federal 42 CFR Part 2 guidelines. Telehealth sessions use our secure electronic health record platform. Information is shared only with people you list on a signed release form, and that includes a spouse or family member.

What if I need medication?

We’ll refer you to a board-certified psychiatrist in the area, or you can choose your own provider.

What if I need to cancel?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance by phone or through the client portal to avoid a $50 fee. EAP users may lose one session for a missed appointment.

What if I have an emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. You can also call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time, day or night.
